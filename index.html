AI Recommendation vs Expert Evaluation

AI ClaimExpert Assessment
40% operating-cost reductionUnsupported. No current-state cost baseline, benefit attribution model or implementation-cost estimate.
Enterprise rollout in 90 daysOver-ambitious. Supplier data quality, ERP integration, controls, process variation and adoption are not assessed.
Autonomous supplier decisionsGovernance gap. Decision rights, approval thresholds, human oversight and exception handling are missing.
Immediate productivity improvementPlausible but unquantified. Baseline cycle time and workload measures are required before benefits can be validated.

Executive Evaluation

The use case has genuine value potential in requisition triage, supplier intelligence, contract knowledge retrieval, exception detection and workflow assistance. However, the proposal confuses technical possibility with enterprise readiness. Approval should therefore be limited to a controlled pilot with measurable baseline and governance gates.

Decision required

Approve a 10–12 week pilot for selected procurement workflows, subject to data-readiness and control assessment.

Critical Strategic Gaps

ROI assumption unsupported
40% savings is presented without baseline, cost model, adoption assumptions or sensitivity analysis.
Data readiness not established
Supplier/master data completeness, duplication, classification and historical transaction quality are unknown.
Integration complexity understated
ERP, procurement, contract, supplier and identity systems require architecture and security assessment.
Human-in-the-loop missing
High-impact supplier and commercial decisions require explicit approval and escalation controls.

Missed Value / Change Considerations

Process redesignAI should simplify work, not automate inefficient process steps unchanged.
Workforce impactRole changes, training and adoption costs are absent.
Value attributionSeparate AI-driven benefits from sourcing policy, demand changes and negotiated savings.
Operating ownershipBusiness owner, model owner, risk owner and support model are undefined.
Scale criteriaNo measurable gate defines when the pilot is ready for broader rollout.

Revised Executive Recommendation

1 • BASELINECurrent cost, cycle time, exception rate and workload.

3 • PILOTLow/medium-risk workflows with clear boundaries.
4 • GOVERNHuman approval, security, audit and escalation controls.

Evaluation Framework

1. Strategic FitDoes the recommendation address a real business priority?
2. Business ValueAre benefits measurable, attributable and material?
3. FeasibilityCan people, process, technology and integrations support it?
4. Data ReadinessIs required data available, usable, governed and sufficiently reliable?
5. Governance & RiskAre decision rights, controls, security, oversight and accountability defined?
6. AdoptionAre operating-model and workforce changes understood?
7. Value RealizationAre baseline, KPIs, benefit owners and scale gates explicit?

Evaluator Mindset

The objective is not to reject AI-generated content because it is imperfect. The evaluator should distinguish a promising idea from an investment-ready recommendation, identify what evidence is missing, expose unrealistic assumptions, and convert the proposal into a safer and more executable strategy.

Core question

“What would need to be true for this recommendation to deliver the claimed business value safely and at scale?”
